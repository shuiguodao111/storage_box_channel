About This Box

Why is it called "Storage Box"?

The Story Behind the Name

My friends call me "Storage Box" because I love collecting and preserving memories. Since childhood, I've always kept diaries, photos, and small souvenirs from important moments.

This nickname stuck with me through university, and now it perfectly represents my mission: to create a digital space where I can store and share the precious moments of my life journey.

What's Inside?

This box contains:

  • My university journey and experiences
  • Precious moments with friends and classmates
  • Beautiful scenery I've encountered
  • Personal growth and learning experiences

My Mission

I believe that memories are our most valuable possessions. Through this website, I want to:

  • Preserve beautiful moments from my university life
  • Share my experiences and journey
  • Create a timeless digital memory box
  • Connect with others through shared experiences
